## 1. Product

**وصل تك | Wasl Tech** is a digital services company serving Yemen and Gulf markets.

Official slogan:
**نبني حضورك الرقمي باحتراف**

The public website is a company/product marketing site whose job is to:
- explain Wasl Tech clearly;
- help visitors identify the right service;
- show verified work as proof;
- explain how projects are approached;
- convert qualified visitors into project conversations;
- support discovery through search engines;
- present the brand with a credible, polished, human-designed experience.

The website is not a generic agency template and is not a marketplace/e-commerce product.

## 2A. Language, discovery and site scope

VNext is designed and generated in **Arabic and English from the same architecture**.

Locale model:
- Arabic is default at `/`;
- English lives under `/en/`;
- entity identity/slugs stay stable across languages;
- Arabic uses RTL;
- English uses LTR.

Public product surfaces include:
- home;
- about;
- services directory;
- service detail pages;
- portfolio;
- project/case-study pages when evidence supports them;
- process;
- contact;
- dedicated project planner;
- FAQ;
- insights/articles;
- careers and job details when real jobs exist;
- privacy policy;
- terms of use;
- 404.

Articles are a real maintained content system for useful search/discovery content, not SEO filler.

Privacy and terms must reflect actual Wasl behavior and tools; they are not copied from competitors.

SEO/discovery is a first-class product goal. Services, projects and articles should form a useful internal-link network so search visitors can enter through the page that best matches their intent.

## 4. Proof / portfolio

Portfolio content is evidence.

Rules:
- use only verified project records/assets;
- each project uses exactly one primary image;
- do not invent conversion/revenue/performance outcomes;
- do not invent client problems, testimonials, duration, or metrics;
- case studies focus on the project summary, context, Wasl contribution, scope and delivered outputs.

Missing fields use `CONTENT REQUIRED` or `NOT VERIFIED`.

## 5. Conversion model

Primary conversion:
**Project planning / structured WhatsApp conversation.**

Secondary channels:
- WhatsApp direct
- email
- social channels where verified

VNext should evolve from a flat contact form into a project planner that can collect:
- needed service / unsure;
- project stage;
- scope priority;
- contact information;
- current URL if any;
- concise brief.

Budget must not be invented. Budget ranges can be added only after Wasl Tech approves real commercial ranges.

## 7. Non-goals

Do not add without a business requirement:
- fake client counters;
- fake awards;
- fake testimonials;
- fake uptime/security guarantees;
- prices;
- delivery-time promises;
- unsupported technology logos;
- a blog filled with SEO filler;
- careers/team pages with invented people/jobs;
- decorative product dashboards that do not exist.

## 8. Success definition

A visitor should be able to answer:
1. What does Wasl Tech build?
2. Which service fits my situation?
3. What does the service include?
4. What proof exists?
5. How does the work progress?
6. How do I start?

The site succeeds when those answers are clear, fast, credible, and easy to act on.
