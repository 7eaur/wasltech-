## Brand implementation constraints
- Preserve SVG proportions/viewBox.
- Do not recolor the primary logo with CSS filters.
- Use the white lockup on dark navigation/surfaces.
- Keep Arabic pages `lang="ar" dir="rtl"`.
- Do not use Teal `#0E8889` for small text on white; use Teal Dark `#096B70`.
- Navy leads; teal is a focused accent.
- Gradients are not a default visual treatment.
- Do not retype or redraw the wordmark.
